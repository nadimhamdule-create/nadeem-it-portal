#001Entra sign-in failureIssue-specific

What is this?

Entra authentication/MFA/Conditional Access/account-access issue.

Common causes

  • Account/password
  • CA policy
  • MFA method
  • Device/risk
  • Token/session

Detailed troubleshooting

Step 1 — Reproduce 'Entra sign-in failure' and capture the exact error, scope, timestamp and last known-good time.
Step 2 — Entra > Identity > Monitoring & health > Sign-in logs: select exact failure.
Step 3 — Open Authentication Details and Conditional Access tabs; record error/correlation ID and failing policy/factor.
Step 4 — Verify account/auth methods/device state; correct only the named policy/factor and confirm a Success sign-in.

Useful commands

dsregcmd /status

Verify resolution
