What is this?

Common causes

  • Routing/compliance
  • DNS auth
  • Spam/quarantine
  • SMTP relay

Detailed troubleshooting

Step 1 — Reproduce 'Gmail user cannot login' and capture the exact error, scope, timestamp and last known-good time.
Step 2 — Admin console > Reporting > Email Log Search: search sender/recipient/time and follow delivery status.
Step 3 — For Groups verify posting/external/moderation; for domain mail verify MX/SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
Step 4 — Check routing/compliance/quarantine only if log shows that path; controlled test and confirm Delivered.
